Limitations

  • Absence of data from a source does not mean the data does not exist; sources have gaps, rate limits and delays.
  • DNS answers depend on the resolver used and can differ by location and time.
  • Subdomains from Certificate Transparency prove a certificate was issued for the name, not that it resolves today.
  • Registration data is self-reported and often redacted; personal contact data is intentionally not collected.
